ABOUT THE SYMPOSIUM

Engineered for Excellence

Organized with pride by the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering, University College of Engineering, Ariyalur.

The POWERNEX 26 Vision

POWERNEX 26 is the annual national level technical symposium designed to challenge, inspire, and elevate engineering students from all technical disciplines across India. Built on the core ethos of electrical innovation, practical circuit debugging, competitive teamwork, and rapid problem-solving, this one-day symposium creates an arena where classroom theory transforms into real-world engineering mastery.

Hosted at the sprawling campus of University College of Engineering, Ariyalur (A Constituent College of Anna University, Chennai), POWERNEX 26 welcomes students from all engineering departments to compete on an equal footing.

Key Highlights

  • All Departments Eligible: Students from all engineering disciplines are warmly invited. Formal EEE background is not required.
  • Anna University Certificates: Official certificates of participation awarded to all registered attendees.
  • No Hidden Costs: Flat Rs. 150 covers all 6 events, welcome kit, ID badge, hot lunch, and refreshments.
  • Overall Championship Trophy: Awarded to the visiting college securing the highest aggregate points across events.
Championship Cup: 1st place earns 10 pts, 2nd earns 7 pts, 3rd earns 5 pts for your college leaderboard!

SYMPOSIUM REGULATIONS

Official Rulebook

Competition framework, general campus code of conduct, and participation guidelines.

GENERAL

1. Identification & College Verification

Every participant must produce a valid College Identity Card and registration confirmation at the verification desk upon arrival. Registration fee is Rs. 150 per participant and is non-refundable. One ticket covers entry to all 6 events, welcome kit, personalized ID card, lunch, refreshments, and Anna University certificates.

  • Strict reporting time: 8:30 AM IST on 08 October 2026.
  • Verification counters close promptly at 9:30 AM for the Inaugural Ceremony.
  • Decent formal or semi-formal academic dress code is mandatory.
GENERAL

2. Authority of Judges & Organizing Committee

The evaluation and decisions made by the judges and faculty committee are final, absolute, and binding. Any argument, dispute, or disruptive behavior will result in immediate team disqualification without refund.

  • Misconduct, tampering with lab apparatus, or cheating leads to immediate expulsion.
  • College property must be respected throughout the campus premises.
TECHNICAL

3. Technical Events Specifications

Paper Presentation: 1 to 3 members. Standard IEEE two-column paper format. Total time: 7 mins presentation + 3 mins Q&A. Bring 2 printed hard copies for the panel and slide deck on a USB drive. Plagiarism above 20% is strictly disqualified.

TechMain: Individual event. Round 1: 30 written MCQs in 25 mins. Round 2: 30 oral speed buzzer questions. Zero negative marking across all rounds. Mobile phones prohibited.

Debugging Circuit: 2 to 4 members. Safe low-voltage DC breadboard circuits only. Teams must bring their own laptop & charger for circuit checks and datasheet references. Internet access is permitted.

NON-TECH

4. Non-Technical Events Specifications

Robo Relay: 2 members per team. 1 performer is blindfolded on the obstacle course; 1 member directs exclusively through voice commands. Strictly no physical contact. 3-strike boundary penalty rule applies.

Mission Impossible: 2 members per team. 5 sequential clue checkpoints across campus. Maximum 3 attempts per clue. The final checkpoint requires 1 member to decrypt digital clues on laptop.

Electro Enigma: 2 members per team. 3 rounds of visual connection, electrical symbols, and cinema technology trivia. Strict 10-second timer per question. Zero negative marking.

LAPTOP

5. Laptop & Internet Regulations

Teams participating in Debugging Circuit and Mission Impossible must bring at least one fully charged laptop with power adapter.

  • Internet access via mobile hotspot is permitted during Debugging Circuit for component datasheets.
  • Dedicated power outlets will be accessible in laboratories.
  • Organizers are not responsible for unattended personal devices; keep belongings secure.
SCHEDULING

6. Morning & Afternoon Block Distribution

Events are structured into two non-clashing blocks so you can participate across categories:

  • Morning Block (10:30 AM - 12:45 PM): Paper Presentation, TechMain & Debugging Circuit
  • Afternoon Block (01:45 PM - 03:30 PM): Debugging Circuit, Robo Relay, Mission Impossible, Electro Enigma
  • Debugging Circuit is accessible across both sessions to provide maximum scheduling flexibility.
